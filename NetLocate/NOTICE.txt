Full page URLs, SHA-256 hashes and filters: Data/regional-manifest.json, embedded.
Builder: tools/Build-RegionalCatalog.ps1 (development only, not part of LIVE).
Host-declared coordinates are not physically verified. Nominal 50 km coordinate
uncertainty is not calibrated coverage. They form a separately labelled conditional
hypothesis and guide search; they never become primary location constraints.
Public metadata is privacy-obfuscated; inbound ICMP reachability is not guaranteed.
Excludes disputed/on-hold metadata, known IPv4 NAT and declared VPN/tunnel/anycast.
Probe stability concerns outbound connectivity, not authentication of inbound replies.
No remote Atlas measurements are commissioned, queried or consumed by the estimator.
